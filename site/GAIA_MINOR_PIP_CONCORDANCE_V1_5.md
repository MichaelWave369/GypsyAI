# GAIA v1.5 · The 40-card Minor Pip Concordance

## What is indexed, and what remains uncertain

This release compares the **four Aces and thirty-six numbered cards (2–10)**
across four historical tarot lineages:

1. Tarot de Marseille (suit/count identity and broad historic pip arrangement).
2. Rider–Waite–Smith (GAIA's existing canonical card names and pictorial minors).
3. Golden Dawn *Book T* (historically indexed elemental Ace root or decan/ruler/Lord title).
4. Crowley/Harris Thoth (distinct printed minor names, including Disks).

GAIA already has **22 Major Arcana** in the main Concordance and
**16 court cards** in the Court Ranks chamber. Together, those **22 + 16 +
40 = 78** cards now have some source-indexed *structural* comparison.

**This is not a claim of 78 independently validated historical meanings.**
The four earlier reviewed Waite card-*interpretation* notes remain a
separate, much smaller research audit. The original GAIA modern
upright/reversed editorial meanings are unchanged.

## The actual differences users can explore

| GAIA / Rider–Waite–Smith | Marseille normalized label | Golden Dawn Book T | Thoth printed label |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ace of Wands | As · Bâtons | Root of the Powers of Fire; Kether, no decan | Ace of Wands |
| 3 of Wands | 3 · Bâtons | Lord of Established Strength; Sun in Aries | 3 of Wands · Virtue |
| 7 of Cups | 7 · Coupes | Lord of Illusionary Success; Venus in Scorpio | 7 of Cups · Debauch |
| 6 of Swords | 6 · Épées | Lord of Earned Success; Mercury in Aquarius | 6 of Swords · Science |
| 7 of Pentacles | 7 · Deniers | Lord of Success Unfulfilled; Saturn in Taurus | 7 of Disks · Failure |
| 3 of Pentacles | 3 · Deniers | Lord of Material Works; Mars in Capricorn | 3 of Disks · Works |

**Marseille warning:** The displayed "3 · Bâtons" / "As · Bâtons"
format is a modern *normalized lookup identifier*, **NOT** a claim
that any surviving Conver, Dodal or Noblet plate carries that
exact printed number or word. Historic Marseille pips usually display
geometric suit symbols and ornament. Certain suits and specific
editions **do not print card numerals**, especially Coins/Deniers.
Rider–Waite–Smith pips typically use specific illustrated scenes
instead. This comparison establishes broad pattern distinction, not
the iconography of every historic card.

**Aces are exceptions:** Book T associates each Ace with Kether and
an elemental Root, not a 10-degree zodiac decan or a single planetary
ruler. The UI and source packet do not invent those values.

**Thoth vs Book T:** Separate short printed keywords in Crowley/Harris
Thoth cannot simply be generated by deleting "Lord of" from the
Book T title: Established Strength ↔ Virtue, Illusionary Success ↔
Debauch, Earned Success ↔ Science, Success Unfulfilled ↔ Failure, etc.
A cited Thoth title index is therefore stored separately in code,
checked against explicit test fixtures, rather than algorithmically
paraphrasing the Golden Dawn.

The source catalog used for Thoth lists **"Works"** for Three of
Disks; some alternate catalogs use **"Work"**. GAIA retains the
"Works" catalog spelling with its matching source and clearly
classifies secondary title transcriptions.

## Sources, with honest evidential roles

### Historical primary texts

- Arthur Edward Waite, *The Pictorial Key to the Tarot* (1911),
  with links to the historical Rider–Waite–Smith deck tradition:
  https://sacred-texts.com/book/the-pictorial-key-to-the-tarot
- *Book T / Liber LXXVIII*, Golden Dawn tradition in *The
  Equinox* I:8 (1912), numbered pips and Aces:
  https://hermeticcloud.com/library/liber-lxxviii/

These establish what particular historical sources **document**;
they do not establish scientific divination effects.

### Modern secondary indexes and comparisons

- Marseille non-scenic pip number and suit arrangement across
  Conver / Dodal / Noblet, including edition-specific missing
  printed numerals:
  https://marselia.app/blog/reading-marseille-tarot-pips
- Thoth full 78-card structural deck index, including "Works":
  https://marselia.app/tarot-decks/thoth
- Alternative Thoth keyword table (possible spelling variants):
  https://billheidrick.com/works/tarottbl.htm

These are **not** original eighteenth-century Marseille plates
or the original 1944 Crowley book. We did not rehost their content
or Harris artwork, and the web is not requested by GAIA when
you browse the data. Links open only when clicked.

## Implementation and privacy

- Original deterministic \`site/src/minorConcordance.ts\` builds
  records from the real bundled 78-card deck. Exactly 40 Ace and
  numbered-pip card IDs are recognized; courts and trumps are
  excluded.
- All 36 pip records must pass \`auditHermeticCard\`'s independently
  source-indexed Golden Dawn field checks. Four Aces retain
  checked Kether/root fields, with explicitly absent decans.
- 36 Thoth short keywords are stored in a distinct curated matrix,
  not inferred from Book T or GAIA's own modern meanings.
- New \`MinorConcordancePanel\`, main navigation doorway \`#pips\`
  and Reading Room entry. Choose a suit and rank, read four side-by-side
  perspectives, open the original card, or inspect typed references.
- Original identities, selection and shuffle logic, Spread Atlas,
  source-labeled Field Loom, historical Scholar Mode, four-note
  interpretation ledger and local Ollama **separate explicit
  consent / Send** remain unchanged.
- No new dependency, model, third-party code/art, server, telemetry,
  browser storage, data export or background network request.
